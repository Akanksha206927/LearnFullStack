About the poet

Hello, I'm Akanksha — a quiet soul with a wandering mind and a heart that finds poetry in the little things.

I find beauty in silence, meaning in fleeting moments and stories in feelings left unspoken. Some emotions are simply too deep to be expressed in ordinary words, so I let poetry speak where I cannot.

Through my words, I capture little pieces of life, untold stories, quiet dreams and emotions that linger long after the moment has passed.

This is my little corner of the universe — a place where thoughts turn into verses, feelings find their way into words and every poem holds a little piece of me.

Stay a while. Perhaps you'll find a piece of yourself in my words. ♡
